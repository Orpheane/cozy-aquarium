before you dive in.

What is Cozy Aquarium?

Cozy Aquarium is a Chrome extension that combines a gamified habit and daily goal tracker with a virtual aquarium. Complete tasks, earn in-game rewards, hatch and care for sea creatures, and personalize your underwater sanctuary.

Can I install Cozy Aquarium now?

The public release is coming soon. The official Chrome Web Store listing may not be accessible to everyone yet. No public release date is announced here.

Who is Cozy Aquarium for?

It is designed for people who enjoy cozy games, collecting creatures, and a playful way to keep track of everyday goals in Chrome. It can suit small self-care tasks, study goals, work tasks, or creative projects.

How do goals connect to the aquarium?

Completing goals earns in-game rewards that help you care for and grow your aquarium. Adventures let you divide bigger goals into smaller milestones.

Can I customize my aquarium?

Yes. Choose your active companions, explore decorations and themes, and customize your profile as you progress.

Does Cozy Aquarium work offline?

The aquarium and its bundled activities can run offline after installation. Chrome Sync and external destinations, including web search, require an internet connection.

Where is my progress stored?

Progress is saved in your browser profile and mirrored through Chrome Sync when available, subject to its storage limits. Backup export, import, and automatic snapshots provide additional ways to keep your progress.

Is this an open-source extension or a download page?

This is a product showcase. The extension's source code and distribution package remain private. When the public release is available, use the official Chrome Web Store listing to install it.
